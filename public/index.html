Use Swop from ChatGPT, Claude & Grok

Look people up by swop.id, browse live prediction markets and creator stores, buy from creators, edit your profile by chatting — and, with your wallet connected, pay, swap, and send in USDC.

Two ways to connect

Available now

Add by URL

Paste Swop's server into ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok as a custom connector. You get the full toolkit, including wallet actions — each gated by your sign-in and the spending caps you set in the Swop app.

In review

Swop directory plugin

Search “Swop” in ChatGPT's plugin directory and add it in one click — discovery and profile editing by chat. Money actions hand off to the Swop app for you to approve.

Add by URL — the endpoint

https://mcp.swopme.co/mcp

In ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Connectors (enable Developer mode if prompted).
  2. Add custom connector.
  3. Paste https://mcp.swopme.co/mcp and connect.
  4. Sign in with Swop when a tool needs your account.

In Claude

  1. Settings → Connectors.
  2. Add custom connector.
  3. Paste https://mcp.swopme.co/mcp and connect.
  4. Approve the Swop sign-in when prompted.

In Grok

  1. Open grok.com/connectors.
  2. New Connector → Custom.
  3. Paste https://mcp.swopme.co/mcp and save.
  4. Sign in with Swop when a tool needs your account.

Public tools (identity, markets, stores) work with no sign-in. Personal tools ask you to sign in with Swop over OAuth; wallet actions additionally respect the caps and consent you set in the app.

What you can do

Discover — no sign-in

  • Resolve a swop.id to its wallet & profile
  • Search people and creators
  • Prediction markets — events, order books, prices, taxonomy
  • Browse a store and get a shareable, agent-payable product link

Manage your Swop — sign in

  • Edit your SmartSite — update your name & bio by chat
  • Create a product — instantly sellable to people and AI agents. Already have a store? Agents can buy from it today, nothing to set up.
  • Check balances & orders

Move money — sign in + your consent

  • Send USDC to any swop.id or address, within your caps
  • Buy from any Swop store — digital items deliver instantly; physical items collect your shipping address and hold payment in escrow until delivery
  • Pay an x402 link from your wallet
  • Get a swap quote or swap on EVM chains
  • Open or close a perps position within your margin & leverage caps

Every money action is bounded by the per-transaction and daily limits you set in the Swop app, and is fully revocable. Nothing moves without your delegation.

Where you stay in control

Swop app → Settings → AI assistants. Turn AI spending on or off, set the per-transaction and daily caps, allow perps trades and token swaps separately, and disconnect any assistant. The same page on the web: swopme.app/ai-assistants.

Linking an account never needs a password in the chat. The assistant opens a Swop sign-in page; approving it in the app finishes the link. Every action an assistant takes shows up in your activity like any other transaction.
